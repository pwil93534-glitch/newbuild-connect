Written 2026-11-16. These are steps for Phillip to do himself; Claude cannot log in to BoldTrail.

Note: when checked on 2026-11-16, phillipknowsaz.com redirected to phillipawilliams.exprealty.com, so that is the BoldTrail site you will be editing.

eXp Expert Care was asked for the exact BoldTrail menu steps on 2026-11-16 and said its sources do not cover them. The steps below follow the usual BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) layout. If a menu name on your screen is different, use the closest match, or ask eXp Expert Care or BoldTrail support: "How do I add a custom link to an outside website in my agent website's menu?"

## The two links to add

| Button text | Web address |
|---|---|
| Real Estate Strategy | https://phillipwilliamsrealestatestrategist.com |
| Get the Book | https://phillipwilliamsrealestate.com |

Type the addresses exactly as shown, starting with `https://`.

## Steps

1. Sign in to BoldTrail (through your eXp sign-in / Okta, or at the BoldTrail login page).
2. In the left menu, open **Website** (it may be called **Website Settings** or **My Website**).
3. Look for **Navigation**, **Menu**, or **Pages**. Open it.
4. Click **Add Link**, **Add Menu Item**, or **Add Custom Link** (wording varies).
5. For the first link:
   - Name / label: `Real Estate Strategy`
   - Type: **External link** (or "Custom URL")
   - URL: `https://phillipwilliamsrealestatestrategist.com`
   - If there is an "Open in new tab/window" box, tick it.
6. Save.
7. Repeat steps 4–6 for `Get the Book` → `https://phillipwilliamsrealestate.com`.
8. If you can choose where the links go, put them in the top menu. If there is also a footer link section, add them there too.
9. Open phillipknowsaz.com in a private/incognito window and click each new link to check it opens the right site.

## Rules to keep in mind while you are in there

- Do not use "Real Estate Group" anywhere in the link text or page text.
- Leave the eXp branding and disclosures BoldTrail adds as they are.
- If you can't find a way to add an outside link, a simple option is to add one line of text with both links to your **About** or **Bio** section instead.
