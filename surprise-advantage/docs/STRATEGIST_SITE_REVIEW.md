## Suggested improvements (in priority order)
1. **Add the four buyer types.** The handoff calls for distinct paths for Veterans, First-Time Buyers, 55+ and Relocation. The site has only Buy and Sell. Suggest four cards on the home page (and on the Buy page), each a short "what I help with" paragraph and a "Schedule a call" button. Copy would be general process language, no statistics.
2. **VA and loan wording on the Buy page needs a disclaimer.** The calculator shows "VA Advantage Active: $0 down payment • No PMI • Competitive rates". "$0 down" is a credit-advertising trigger term, and VA eligibility and the VA funding fee apply. Suggest: "VA loans may allow no down payment for eligible borrowers. Eligibility, the VA funding fee, and lender requirements apply." Plus a line under the calculator: "Estimate only: principal and interest. Does not include taxes, insurance, HOA, or mortgage insurance. Not a loan offer. Talk to a licensed lender." Worth a quick check with eXp compliance.
3. **Assumable loan section overpromises.** The heading says "Assumable Loan Finder" but there is no finder tool, and the copy says "Save thousands" and "More equity from day one. Build wealth faster." Suggest renaming to "Assumable Loans" and softening to "Some VA and FHA loans can be assumed. We'll review whether a home's existing loan is assumable and what the lender requires."
4. **Market page is thin.** "What's Actually Happening in the West Valley Market?" is followed by three general tips and no market facts. Options: link to the book site's Market Brief, or rename to "How to Read the Market". Any numbers added later need a source and an as-of date.
5. **Feature the book on the home page.** The nav and footer now link to it (preview), but a short section near the bottom ("Read The Surprise Advantage — request a copy") would help, since the book is the main lead offer.
6. **Small clean-ups.**
   - Home page closing section links to the strategist site itself in a new tab; remove or replace with "Search Homes".
   - "Elite Negotiation" and "Timeless Results" are fine as style, but avoid adding any claims that imply results or rankings.
