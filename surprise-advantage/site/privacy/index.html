This policy explains what information The Surprise Advantage website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The site is operated by Phillip Williams, a licensed Arizona real estate agent with eXp Realty (Phillip Williams Real Estate Group), 16165 N 83rd Avenue, Peoria, AZ 85382.

What we collect

  • What you type into a form: your name, email address and mailing address (to send a book you request), the topics you choose (buying, selling, relocating, investing), and any optional message.
  • Booking a conversation: your name, email, phone number, the time you pick, and anything you add to the booking form.
  • Your choices: each permission you give or decline, and the date you gave it.
  • Where you came from: the page or campaign (for example a QR code on the book) that brought you to a form, and the date you submitted it.
  • Email activity: if you receive our emails, we may see whether a link was clicked.
  • Replies: anything you send us by email or text.

This site does not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

How we use it

  • Your name and address: to mail the book you asked for and send a delivery confirmation.
  • Your email: only for what you separately agreed to, such as follow-up emails from Phillip after you request the book, or the monthly market update.
  • Your topics: to send relevant material. Choosing a topic is never treated as permission to market to you.
  • Booking details: to hold and confirm the conversation you scheduled. Booking a conversation is not permission for marketing emails or texts.

Email messages

Every email permission is a separate checkbox that starts unticked. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and we also honor unsubscribe requests sent to us directly. Opting out stops all marketing emails from us.

You will receive text messages from us only if you have explicitly opted in, and we keep a dated record of that choice. Texts, if you choose to receive them, relate to the book you requested, appointment reminders, and replies to your questions. We do not send promotional texts without your separate consent. We follow the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and other applicable laws.

We do not share text-message opt-in information, phone numbers or consent records with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Our messaging provider and carriers handle this information only to deliver the messages you asked for.
