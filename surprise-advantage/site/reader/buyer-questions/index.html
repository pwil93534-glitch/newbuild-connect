Buyer Questions Checklist

Questions worth asking before you tour, make an offer or sign a contract.

About you

  • What monthly payment is comfortable, not just approvable?
  • How long do you expect to stay?
  • What are your must-haves versus nice-to-haves?

About financing

  • Have you spoken with more than one lender?
  • What are the full costs: down payment, closing costs, reserves?
  • What loan options might fit your situation?

About the home and community

  • What are the HOA fees and rules?
  • What are the property tax and insurance estimates?
  • What does the inspection process look like, and what happens if issues are found?

About the agreement

  • What contingencies and deadlines apply?
  • What is included in the sale and what is not?
  • Who represents whom, and how is each party paid?
