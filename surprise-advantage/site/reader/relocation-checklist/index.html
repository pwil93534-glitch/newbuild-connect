Relocation Checklist

Questions and tasks for families moving to Surprise and the West Valley.

Before you visit

  • Define your timeline and budget.
  • List what matters: schools, commute, climate, community, amenities.
  • Plan a visit that includes ordinary weekday and weekend routines.

While you visit

  • Drive your real commute at the times you would travel.
  • Visit neighborhoods at different times of day.
  • Ask each community about HOA terms and upcoming development.

Making the move

  • Decide how to handle selling and buying timelines.
  • Research utilities, vehicle registration and licensing requirements.
  • Arrange movers, temporary housing if needed, and a plan for the first month.
