Seller Preparation Checklist

A calm, step-by-step checklist for the months before listing a home.

Clarify your goals

  • Why are you selling, and by when?
  • What do you need the sale to accomplish financially and personally?
  • Where will you live next, and when do you need to be there?

Understand your numbers

  • Request your current mortgage payoff amount.
  • List repairs, upgrades and any known issues.
  • Ask for a written estimate of selling costs from your agent.

Prepare the home

  • Gather permits, warranties, manuals and HOA documents.
  • Complete or price out necessary repairs.
  • Declutter and deep-clean; plan for photos and showings.

Choose your approach

  • Interview more than one agent; ask how they would market your home.
  • Ask how they would measure and report progress.
  • Review the listing agreement carefully before signing.
